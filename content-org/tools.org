Stuff I actually send people, and a few things I just live in.

The [[https://rgoswami.me/posts/rg-collection-weblinks/][weblinks]] page is
the pile of places that mention me. This one is shorter. It is what I
hand someone when we need a time, a clock, or a scribble, and then the
things older posts already named, corrected where I have moved on.

** Scheduling
When a group needs a time I send [[https://crab.fit/][crab.fit]]. I paint
the hours I can make, they paint theirs, and you can watch the overlap
fill in. [[https://github.com/GRA0007][Ben Grant]] wrote it, ~GPLv3~, and
the [[https://github.com/GRA0007/crab.fit][repository]] is the whole
story. A name can have a password, but that only stops someone else
editing that one row. Leave an event alone for three months and it goes
away, which is fine. No ads. [[http://whenisgood.net/][WhenIsGood]] is
the same grid and I stopped sending it because of the ads.

** Countdown
The timer on a slide is Garrick Aden-Buie's [[https://pkg.garrickadenbuie.com/countdown/][countdown]]
package. ~countdown(minutes = 5)~, click or space to start, double-click
or escape to reset. If the room does not have R open, the same clock is
full screen at [[https://apps.garrickadenbuie.com/countdown/][apps.garrickadenbuie.com/countdown]].

** Scribbles
[[https://jamboard.google.com/][Jamboard]] is gone. Google turned the old
jams into PDFs in 2025, which is an archive, not a board.
[[https://whiteboardfox.com/][Whiteboard Fox]] looked like the
replacement (a room lands on a host like
[[https://r8.whiteboardfox.com/][r8.whiteboardfox.com]], that ~r8~ is not
a code) but the free boards carry ads, so I don't send those. What I
send is [[https://wbo.ophir.dev/][WBO]]. Make a private board, send the
link, the drawing stays. The code is
[[https://github.com/lovasoa/whitebophir][whitebophir]], and there are
no ads. A scribble I want on my own machine afterwards is still
[[https://github.com/xournalpp/xournalpp][Xournal++]], same as the
[[https://rgoswami.me/posts/multi-monitor-touch/][touchscreen post]].
